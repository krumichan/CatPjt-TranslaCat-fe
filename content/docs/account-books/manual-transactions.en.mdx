---
title: Enter income or expenses manually
description: Record income or expenses without a receipt photo.
---

Start by [opening an account book](/en/docs/account-books/open).

## Follow these steps

1. Select **Add transaction (거래 등록)** near the top of the book details.
2. Under **Manual entry (직접 입력)**, choose **Expense (지출)** or **Income (수입)**.
3. Enter or check the **Transaction name (거래명)**, **Category (카테고리)**, **Amount (금액)**, and **Transaction date (거래일)**. Enter the amount in the book's base currency.
4. Optionally add a **Store (점포명)** or **Income source (수입처)** and a **Memo (메모)**. Categories and stores can be selected from existing entries or entered manually.

5. Select **Save (등록)**. To change an existing transaction, open **Edit (수정)**, make your changes, and select **Save (저장)**.

## Check the result

Check the new transaction's name, amount, and date in the list. Make sure the selected period includes the transaction date.
