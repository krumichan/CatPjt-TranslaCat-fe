title: Invite members and share a book
description: Invite members and manage account book invitations, membership, and owner permissions.
---

Start by [opening an account book](/en/docs/account-books/open).

## Follow these steps

1. As the owner, select the **People icon** in the book list or **Manage members (멤버 관리)** in the book details.
2. Enter the other person's **Public ID** and select **Invite (초대)**.
3. When they accept under **Invitations (초대)** in the notification center at the top, they join the book as a member.

## Manage membership and permissions

The creator is the **Owner (오너)**. People who accept invitations join as **Members (멤버)**.

| Task | How to do it |
| --- | --- |
| Invite a member | The owner opens the people icon or **Manage members (멤버 관리)**, enters the person's **Public ID**, and selects **Invite (초대)**. |
| Accept or decline an invitation | The recipient opens **Invitations (초대)** in the top notification center and accepts or declines the account book invitation. |
| Cancel a pending invitation or remove a member | The owner uses **Pending invitations (초대 대기)** or **Current members (현재 멤버)** in member management. |
| Edit the book name, description, or category | The owner selects the card's pencil icon, edits the values, and selects **Save (등록)**. Saving with this month's goal amount empty removes that month's goal. |
| Leave a book | A member selects **Leave account book (가계부 나가기)** in the book details and confirms. After leaving, they can no longer view that book. |
| Delete a book | The owner selects the card's trash icon and confirms. |

Only the owner can edit or delete the book itself and manage members. If these controls are missing, check your role. See [Profile and settings](/en/docs/settings) to find your Public ID.

## Check the result

After sending an invitation, check that the person appears under pending invitations. Once they accept, check the current member list.
