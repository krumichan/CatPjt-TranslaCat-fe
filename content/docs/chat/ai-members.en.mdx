You can read these instructions without signing in. To use the feature, [sign in](/en/login) with your own account. Service links open the English interface. Screenshots show the Korean interface; Korean labels in parentheses help you compare the two.

## Talk to an AI member

An owner or room admin can add AI members to group and OPEN rooms. A room with AI displays an **AI disclosed / AI undisclosed** notice according to its identification policy. In an undisclosed room, individual members may not be labeled as AI.

To call an AI directly, type **@ followed by its actual nickname**. For example, if its nickname is **미케**, write **@미케 Suggest an expression to practice today**. The room's invocation permissions and usage limits may prevent a response. If autonomous participation or proactive speech is enabled, the AI can join the conversation without a direct mention.

## Manage your room's AI settings

Owners and room admins can open **⋮ menu → AI members and room settings**.

1. Under **Add AI member**, enter a nickname, source language, and **Persona prompt**. Add an introduction, profile image, or background image if wanted.
2. **Save**, then check the member list. Follow the displayed current and maximum AI member counts.
3. Under **Room AI policies**, choose individual AI disclosure, **@AI invocation permissions**, **autonomous participation**, and **room activation speech**. Changing a policy starts its save operation.
4. Existing AI members can be **edited / deleted**. Deleting an AI member leaves messages it already sent.

Limits are 50 characters for the nickname, 200 for the introduction, and 4,000 for the Persona prompt. Profile images support JPG, PNG, and WEBP up to 5 MB; background images support the same formats up to 10 MB. After a save or image-processing error, reload and check what was actually applied.

AI replies and translations can be wrong. Responses depend on room settings, invocation limits, and server processing, so an AI does not necessarily answer every message.

## Check that you are done

Check that the AI nickname appears in the list. Wait for policy saving to finish. If an error appears, reload to check the actual values. Use that nickname when mentioning the AI in conversation.
