You can read these instructions without signing in. To use the feature, [sign in](/en/login) with your own account. Service links open the English interface. Screenshots show the Korean interface; Korean labels in parentheses help you compare the two.

## Open a direct chat with a friend

1. Find the friend in your [friends list](/en/chat?tab=friends).
2. Click **1:1 chat (1:1 채팅)**. An already-friended search result also offers **Start 1:1 chat**.
3. Check the room name and the other person's profile before chatting.

If you are not friends yet, [add the friend](/en/docs/chat/friends) first.

## Create a group and invite members

### Start from the friends list

1. In **Friends**, click **Select for group (그룹 선택)**.
2. Select at least one friend and click **Create group (그룹 만들기)**.
3. Check the selected friends and enter a **group name**. The **description** is optional.
4. Click **Create group chat (그룹 채팅 만들기)** to enter the new room.

Use **Change friend selection** to adjust the selection, or **Clear selection** in the friends list to remove all selections. Users whose blocking or friend status changed may be excluded during final confirmation.

### Invite from an existing room

In a room that permits invitations, open **⋮ room menu → Invite members**. Select friends or use **Enter TranslaCat ID directly → Add** to add invitation targets.

| Current room | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Direct chat with a friend | Enter a group name and click **Create group**. A **new group room** is created with the existing partner and invitees. The original direct room and messages remain; messages are not copied to the new group. |
| Regular group chat | The owner or a room admin can add members with **Invite**. |

You cannot invite yourself, someone already in the room, or a user in a blocked relationship. The invite button may be unavailable for your room type or role.

## Check that you are done

Check that the new room opens with the intended name and members. After inviting to an existing group, check the added users in its member list.
