## If something goes wrong

| Situation | Check first |
| --- | --- |
| You cannot find a friend | Enter their exact TranslaCat ID rather than their nickname. |
| You cannot start a direct chat | Check that the friend request was accepted and neither user has blocked the other. |
| A message will not send | Check the connection and your room participation status. You cannot send in an ended OPEN room or one from which you were removed. |
| A translation is missing | Check your incoming translation language and translation display setting. Retry a failed translation with **Retranslate**. |
| Invite or AI controls are missing | Available menus depend on the room type and your role. |
| An AI does not answer your mention | Check its actual nickname, the room's invocation permissions, and usage limits. |
| Rooms or older messages will not load | Click **Retry** in the error area and check your network and sign-in status. |

For further help, see the [customer support guide](/en/docs/support) and include the room type and displayed error.
