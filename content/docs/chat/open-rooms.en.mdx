## Join an OPEN chat

OPEN chat lets you participate without a friend relationship, using a profile specific to that room.

1. Click **Browse OPEN chat (OPEN 채팅 둘러보기)** on the chat screen.
2. Search by room name or description, or browse the public room list. Clear the search to return to all public rooms.
3. Open **View details** for a room and check its description, owner, member count, and availability.
4. Click **Join (참여하기)** and choose a **room nickname** and optional profile image.
5. Check the participation profile and click **Join with this profile**. If you already joined, use **Enter chat room** instead.

A room-specific profile is separate from your normal profile. OPEN screens do not show your normal profile, TranslaCat ID, or friend relationships. Members with the same nickname are distinguished by a unique **member code**, which cannot be changed.

Profile images support **JPG, PNG, and WEBP up to 5 MB**. Without an image, the default OPEN avatar is used. If joining succeeded but image processing failed, use **Retry image processing**.

You cannot join if the room is full, closed, or participation is restricted. **Unlisted (link-only)** rooms do not appear in lists or search. Open the shared room link or use **Preview room** in a received invitation.

## Open a room from an invitation

1. Select [**OPEN chat invitations**](/en/chat/open/invitations) at the top of [Chat](/en/chat). You can also open the room preview from an OPEN chat invitation in the notification center.
2. Check the room name and the inviter's room nickname, then select **Preview room**. If you only want to acknowledge the invitation, select **Mark read**.
3. To join, use the join button on the preview and confirm your room profile. Receiving the invitation or previewing the room does not join it automatically.

## Edit your OPEN profile or leave

- Inside the room, use **⋮ menu → Edit my OPEN profile** to change the room nickname and image, then **Save changes**. This does not affect your normal profile.
- Open a member profile to view and copy its member code.
- **Leave OPEN room** opens a confirmation. Your profile and member code are retained when you leave and rejoin, but **messages from before rejoining are no longer available to you**.
- If the owner ends the room, messages can no longer be sent. If you were removed, you cannot rejoin until the room's block is lifted.

## Check that you are done

The room conversation should open with your room-specific profile. If you edited the profile, reopen it after saving to check the changes.
