You can read these instructions without signing in. To use the feature, [sign in](/en/login) with your own account. Service links open the English interface. Screenshots show the English interface with example data. Korean labels in parentheses can help if you use the Korean interface.

## Open chat

<Tabs items={["PC", "Mobile"]} groupId="guide-device-en">
  <Tab value="PC">
    1. Click **Chat (채팅)** in the left menu, or open the [chat screen](/en/chat).
    2. Use the **Chat (채팅)** tab for rooms you have joined and the **Friends (친구)** tab for your friends.
    3. Inside a room, open **My language settings (내 언어 설정)** at the top for translation languages. The **⋮ button** opens the room menu.

    <GuideScreenshot src="/images/guides/en/chat-pc.jpg" alt="Desktop chat screen with Chat Friends and OPEN chat navigation" title="PC: start with rooms or friends" caption="English interface with example data. Choose a joined room or start another conversation through Friends or OPEN chat." width={1280} height={900} step={1} locale="en" />
  </Tab>
  <Tab value="Mobile">
    1. Tap **☰ menu → Chat (채팅)** at the top left, or open the [chat screen](/en/chat).
    2. Switch between **Chat / Friends (채팅 / 친구)** and scroll the list to find a room or friend.
    3. On a narrow room screen, **My language settings** becomes a **language icon** at the top right. Tap it for the same settings. The adjacent **⋮ button** opens the room menu.

    <GuideScreenshot src="/images/guides/en/chat-mobile.jpg" alt="Mobile chat screen with Chat Friends and OPEN chat navigation" title="Mobile: switch lists using the tabs" caption="English interface with example data. Scroll to see buttons for new conversations and the room list." width={390} height={844} step={1} locale="en" />
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

Menus and buttons adapt to the screen width. Use the mobile instructions if your PC window is narrow.

Select **User guide** next to the Chat title to open the full chat guide in a new tab. In a room, use the **?** in the language settings or **⋮** menu to open the guide for that task directly.

Use [**OPEN chat invitations**](/en/chat/open/invitations) at the top of Chat to see received invitations. Choose **Preview room** to look at the room before deciding whether to join.

## Check that you are done

You are ready when you can find the Chat/Friends tabs and OPEN chat entry button. Select a room in the list to continue an existing conversation.
