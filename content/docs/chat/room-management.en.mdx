You can read these instructions without signing in. To use the feature, [sign in](/en/login) with your own account. Service links open the English interface. Screenshots show the Korean interface; Korean labels in parentheses help you compare the two.

## Open the OPEN chat screen

1. From [Chat](/en/chat), click **Browse OPEN chat (OPEN 채팅 둘러보기)**.
2. Click **Create a new OPEN chat (새 OPEN 채팅 만들기)** to enter room details.

## Create your OPEN room

1. Click **Create a new OPEN chat**.
2. Enter a **room name** of at most 100 characters and **description** of at most 500 characters. Both are required.
3. Choose **visibility**. **Public** rooms appear in lists and search. **Unlisted (link-only)** rooms can be opened by users who have the link. Private rooms requiring admission approval are not supported.
4. Choose **maximum members** between 2 and 100. The initial value is 50.

<GuideScreenshot src="/images/guides/ko/chat-open-create.jpg" alt="OPEN room creation form with name description visibility and capacity" title="Step 2: enter room details and visibility" caption="Korean interface with example data. 5. Set the **OWNER room-specific profile**. The **room nickname** is separate from your normal profile. Optionally choose an image with **Select image**. JPG, PNG, and WEBP up to 5 MB are supported; otherwise the default OPEN avatar is used.

6. Check the room information and room-specific profile, then click **Create room (방 만들기)**. You join the created room as its owner (OWNER).

If the room was created but only image or profile saving failed, use **Retry remaining profile setup**. You do not need to create the same room again.

## Owner and room-admin controls

The following controls depend on your role in the room. Some are unavailable to regular members.

- Copy the room link with **Share link**.
- In member management, the owner can **Assign admin / Remove admin**. The owner and room admins can **Remove** permitted targets. Enter the removal reason and confirm.
- In **Blacklist management**, search by nickname or member code and **Unblock** entries you have permission to manage. Unblocking does not automatically rejoin the member; they must join again.
- An owner with other active members uses **Transfer OWNER and leave** to choose a new owner. If the owner is alone, **End OPEN room** is offered. Ending a room cannot be undone, so read the confirmation before continuing.

## Check that you are done

Check that you entered the room as OWNER with the intended visibility, capacity, and room profile. After member management, check the member list or blacklist too.
