Screenshots use example data in English or Korean, as indicated in the captions. Korean labels in parentheses can help if you use the Korean interface.

## Open the OPEN chat screen

1. From [Chat](/en/chat), click **Browse OPEN chat (OPEN 채팅 둘러보기)**.
2. Click **Create a new OPEN chat (새 OPEN 채팅 만들기)** to enter room details.

## Create your OPEN room

1. Click **Create a new OPEN chat**.
2. Enter a **room name** of at most 100 characters and **description** of at most 500 characters. Both are required.
3. Choose **visibility**. **Public** rooms appear in lists and search. **Unlisted (link-only)** rooms can be opened by users who have the link. Private rooms requiring admission approval are not supported.
4. Choose **maximum members** between 2 and 100. The initial value is 50.

<GuideScreenshot src="/images/guides/ko/chat-open-create.jpg" alt="OPEN room creation form with name description visibility and capacity" title="Step 2: enter room details and visibility" caption="Korean interface with example data. The form contains sample draft values and has not been submitted. Enter your own name, description, visibility, and capacity." width={1280} height={900} step={2} locale="en" />

5. Set the **OWNER room-specific profile**. The **room nickname** is separate from your normal profile. Optionally choose an image with **Select image**. JPG, PNG, and WEBP up to 5 MB are supported; otherwise the default OPEN avatar is used.

6. Check the room information and room-specific profile, then click **Create room (방 만들기)**. You join the created room as its owner (OWNER).

If the room was created but only image or profile saving failed, use **Retry remaining profile setup**. You do not need to create the same room again.

## Owner and room-admin controls

The following controls depend on your role in the room. Some are unavailable to regular members.

- Copy the room link with **Share link**.
- In member management, the owner can **Assign admin / Remove admin**. The owner and room admins can **Remove** permitted targets. Enter the removal reason and confirm.
- In **Blacklist management**, search by nickname or member code and **Unblock** entries you have permission to manage. Unblocking does not automatically rejoin the member; they must join again.

### Invite someone by TranslaCat ID

1. In an OPEN room you have joined, open **⋮ → Invite by TranslaCat ID**.
2. Enter the recipient's exact [TranslaCat ID](/en/docs/settings/profile) and select **Send invitation**. This is different from the member code shown in the OPEN room.
3. The recipient can preview the room from [OPEN chat invitations](/en/chat/open/invitations) and decide whether to join. Sending an invitation does not add them automatically.

### Transfer ownership or leave

- To stay in the room, select **Transfer owner**, choose an active member, and confirm. That member becomes the new owner, and you stay in the room as a regular member.
- To transfer and leave, select **Transfer OWNER and leave**. If the transfer succeeds but leaving fails, retry with **Leave OPEN room**.
- If there are no other active members, you cannot transfer ownership. **Close OPEN room** is available when the owner is the only member left. A closed room cannot be restored, and members can no longer send messages.

## Check that you are done

Check that you entered the room as OWNER with the intended visibility, capacity, and room profile. After member management, check the member list or blacklist too.
