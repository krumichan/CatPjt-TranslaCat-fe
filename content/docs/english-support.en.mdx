Use English for conversation translation, language learning and novels you have permission to read. **Each feature has its own language settings**, so check the feature you want to use.

<Callout title="Guide language and service language">
  Guides are available in Korean, Japanese and English. The service also has an **English interface**: open **Language → EN (English)**. Service links here open English pages, while screenshots show the Korean interface. Interface language, guide language and each feature's study or translation language are separate settings. You can read this guide without signing in. Sign in to use features; novel translation requires an ADMIN account.
</Callout>

## Voice translation between Korean, Japanese and English

The three languages support **six directions**: Korean→Japanese, Japanese→Korean, Korean→English, English→Korean, Japanese→English and English→Japanese.

1. Open [voice translation](/en/voice) and choose the input mode. Set **Input language (입력 언어)** to automatic detection or specify the language actually being spoken.
2. Set **Translation language (번역 언어)** to the language you want to read. To read Korean speech in English, choose Korean input and English translation.
3. Start the session and check original text, translations and connection status. In Meeting mode, check the input language for your voice and the other person's voice separately.

When the speech already matches the translation language, only the original text may appear because translation is unnecessary. See [input modes](/en/docs/voice/input-modes) for microphone, system audio and PC/mobile differences, or [live translation](/en/docs/voice/live-translation) for starting and ending a session.

## Chat: room settings and defaults

Open **My language settings (내 언어 설정)** at the top of a room, choose **Original text language** and **Incoming translation language**, then save. On mobile, use the language icon. Choose English as the source when typing in English, or as the incoming language to read other people's messages in English.

Room settings apply to your view. Set starting values for other rooms in [profile chat defaults](/en/docs/settings/chat-defaults); saved room-specific choices take precedence. An **AI member's language** is a separate setting managed by the owner or a room admin. See [AI members](/en/docs/chat/ai-members).

## Learning: effective dates and your current language pair

In [English learning settings](/en/docs/language-learning/english), choose a study language different from your source language. To study English, set **Learning language to English (영어)** and choose a feedback language you can read comfortably. Initial settings apply immediately. Later language, timezone and daily-goal changes apply from the next learning day in your currently active learning timezone.

Use the level test and [writing](/en/docs/language-learning/writing), [speaking](/en/docs/language-learning/speaking), [listening](/en/docs/language-learning/listening) and [reading](/en/docs/language-learning/reading) in your active language. For web AI, also check the [plan's languages](/en/docs/language-learning/web-ai). The Korean-to-Japanese QA writing exercise remains specific to that pair; English learning uses general writing.

Current scores and profiles use your source/study language pair, while **whole history and consecutive learning days preserve activities across languages**. The daily completion limit for official level tests is shared across languages. Base Level and Band are internal learning scales, not CEFR grades or official exam results.

## Administrator-only English novel translation

<Callout title="Available accounts and works">
  Novel translation requires an **ADMIN account**. English translation supports works from **Syosetu (小説家になろう)**. Kakuyomu does not offer the English translation selector. Regular accounts cannot access the novel menu or screens.
</Callout>

1. Sign in with an ADMIN account, open [Novel translation (소설 번역)](/en/novel), choose **Syosetu**, then open a search or ranking list.
2. Choose **Translation language → English (번역 언어 → 영어)** in the list. Open a work and a chapter from its contents, then check the language selector above the reader. A short story may open directly in the reader.
3. If translation is needed, select **Translate the episode (전체 번역 시작)**. Read the prepared **English translation (영어 번역)** and use the offered retranslation controls for failed sections.
4. Use **PREV / NEXT** in the control bar at the bottom of the screen to move between chapters. Use **OPEN → LIST** to return to the contents. These moves and refresh keep your English selection.
5. Expand **OPEN** and select **Read all translation (번역문 전체 읽기)** to hear the prepared English translation. **Read all source text (원문 전체 읽기)** plays Japanese. Pause, resume, stop and reading-speed controls are also available.
6. Later, reopen the work from **Recent activity (최근 활동)** in the side menu to restore its last English selection.

Work titles, descriptions and chapter titles **may remain in Japanese when English metadata is not ready**. Reading the body in English does not automatically translate every title. Read-aloud covers the current chapter's body; it excludes the title, foreword and afterword.

## Check your settings and continue

Confirm that English is selected in the feature you are using. Opening the English guide or changing your study language does not change the languages of chat, voice translation and novels together.
