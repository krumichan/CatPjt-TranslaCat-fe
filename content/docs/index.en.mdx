Expand a feature in the sidebar and choose **the task you want to complete**. Each page walks you through one task, such as creating an account book or reviewing a receipt.

Use the **PC / Mobile** tabs where the layouts differ. Follow the mobile instructions on a tablet or narrow desktop window if you see the menu button. Select a screenshot to enlarge it. Names and records shown in feature screenshots are sample data.

<Callout title="English guides and English learning">
  You are reading the English guide. The service offers an **English interface**: open the **Language** menu and choose **EN (English)**. Service links in this guide open English pages. Screenshots show the Korean interface with example data. **Study and translation languages** are separate choices from the interface and guide languages.
</Callout>

This guide is public. You need to sign in to use the service or submit a support inquiry.
