TranslaCat has an **English interface** as well as English learning and translation options. To change menus and buttons, choose **EN (English)** in the **Language** menu. Your study language and translation targets are set separately within each feature.

## Set up English learning

1. Open **Language learning → Settings (언어 학습 → 설정)** in the [English service interface](/en/language-learning/settings). On mobile, use Settings at the top of the learning screen.
2. Choose Korean or Japanese as your **Source language (원문 언어)** and **English (영어)** as your **Learning language (학습 언어)**. Source and learning languages must differ.
3. Choose a **Feedback language (설명·피드백 언어)**. For example, you can learn English while reading explanations in Korean.
4. Review your daily goals, timezone and voice defaults, then select **Save settings (설정 저장)**.

Your first settings take effect immediately. Later changes to **languages, timezone and daily goals** take effect on the next learning day in your currently active learning timezone. Check the effective date under pending settings. Questions already created for today retain the settings used when they were created. **Default voice, playback speed and Listening task types** save immediately as defaults for subsequent use.

## Check your English level

1. Confirm that your changed language is active, then open the [level test](/en/language-learning/level-test).
2. If a test is required for a new language pair, follow the on-screen steps. A test contains 20 questions covering vocabulary, grammar, reading, listening, writing and speaking.
3. Review the **Base Level** and measured activities. Japanese results do not become your English level.

<Callout title="An internal learning scale">
  TranslaCat uses a base score and the internal bands FOUNDATION, BASIC, INTERMEDIATE, UPPER_INTERMEDIATE and ADVANCED. These are not CEFR grades or official exam results. The daily completion limit for official level tests is shared across languages; switching languages does not reset that day's limit.
</Callout>

## Practice and review your records

- Use general [writing practice](/en/docs/language-learning/writing) for your active English language pair. The Korean-to-Japanese QA exercise is specific to that pair; it is not an English exercise.
- Check the active language before starting [speaking](/en/docs/language-learning/speaking), [listening](/en/docs/language-learning/listening) or [reading](/en/docs/language-learning/reading). Their guides explain evaluation, coaching and audio controls.
- The dashboard, current profile, recent level test and daily writing use your active source/learning language pair. Earlier Japanese learning records are retained; check the activity details in [history](/en/docs/language-learning/history).
  Overall learning history and learning streaks retain activities across languages, so not every statistic is separated by language.
- A [web AI plan](/en/docs/language-learning/web-ai) has its own source, learning and feedback languages. Check the plan as well when preparing English practice.

## Check completion

Your active settings should show **English**, and a newly started activity should use English. If Japanese still appears, first check the pending effective date and the language of any questions already created for today.

## Next steps

Conversation translation has separate settings. Choose English in [room language settings](/en/docs/chat/messages-translation), [chat defaults](/en/docs/settings/chat-defaults) or [voice translation languages](/en/docs/voice/languages-storage) as needed.
