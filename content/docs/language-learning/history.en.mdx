## Find a record

1. Open [learning history](/en/language-learning/history). On PC, choose **Learning history (학습 이력)** at the top; on mobile, tap **History (이력)**.
2. Select the activity and period. Listening also offers a learning-method filter.
3. Select a record and read its answers, evaluation, and feedback.
4. Retry only questions that offer a review button, within the stated allowance.

## History and accumulated learning evidence

**Learning history** combines Writing, Speaking, Listening, Reading, level tests, web AI, and existing Vocabulary records. Select an activity and date range, then open a record. Listening can be filtered further by learning method.

On PC, the list and detail appear side by side. On mobile, details follow the list, so scroll down after selecting a record. Review answers, evaluation, and feedback. You can retry only when a review button is offered.

**Accumulated learning evidence** lets you query saved records by start/end date, activity, language, result type, and other conditions. The maximum date range is 366 days. This search does not create a new evaluation. Click **Reopen original learning and feedback** for the details that remain available.

Read coaching separately from scored evaluation. If the source record was deleted or audio retention has expired, some content may no longer be available. A failed query does not mean no records exist; use **Retry** or refresh.

## Check that you are done

Check the date and activity of the record you found, then read its feedback. If the list is empty, broaden the filters. Check the processing status of records still being evaluated.
