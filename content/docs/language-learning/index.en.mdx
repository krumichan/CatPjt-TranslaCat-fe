## Open the learning screen

<Tabs items={["PC", "Mobile"]} groupId="guide-device-en">
  <Tab value="PC">
    1. Click **Language learning (언어 학습)** in the left menu, or its card in the service list.
    2. Open **Today's practice (오늘의 학습)** at the top and choose **Writing / Speaking / Listening / Reading**.
    3. Open **Learning history (학습 이력)** for records, or **Settings (설정)** for study languages and goals.

    <GuideScreenshot src="/images/guides/ko/language-learning-pc.jpg" alt="Desktop learning dashboard and activity navigation in Korean" title="PC: choose an activity from the top menu" caption="Korean interface with example data.   <Tab value="Mobile">
    1. Open the **☰ menu** at the top left and tap **Language learning (언어 학습)**.
    2. Tap **Practice (학습)** at the top of the learning screen, then choose **Writing / Speaking / Listening / Reading**.
    3. Use **Home / History / Settings (홈 / 이력 / 설정)** for the dashboard, saved records, and learning settings.

A narrow PC window can use the mobile layout. The learning content and submission steps are the same; on mobile, scroll between questions and answer fields.

## Learning English

In [learning settings](/en/docs/language-learning/settings-history), choose **English** as your study language and a different source language. Save the settings, check when they take effect, and follow any request to complete a level test. Choosing the English guide does not change your account's study language or the application's display language.

## Read the dashboard

**Integrated language ability** shows measured abilities and the scope of evaluation. **Performance by activity** shows recent scores and today's progress. In the trend view, choose a period and activity. When there are too few records, the screen says it is **collecting data**.

Your **learning profile** contains a baseline level, strengths and weaknesses, and suggested study directions. Use **Expand** or **Expand all** on collapsed sections. The profile may be adjusted as actual learning results accumulate.

## Check completion and continue learning

After submitting an answer, check its evaluation status. Reopen saved results in [learning history](/en/docs/language-learning/history). If preparation or evaluation fails, or a usage limit appears, follow that task's retry instructions.

If the screen asks you to finish settings or a level test, use the provided button first. You can start with questions that are already ready while the others are prepared. If the remaining preparation stops, use its retry button. If a feature is paused or you have reached today's limit, follow the on-screen guidance and choose another activity or return later.
