[Speaking](/en/language-learning/speaking) lets you send recorded answers and practice with AI. On PC, choose **Today's practice → Speaking**; on mobile, choose **Practice → Speaking**.

<Callout title="Before you begin">You can read this guide without signing in. Sign in to use the feature with your own account. Service links open the English interface. Screenshots show the Korean interface with example data; Korean labels in parentheses help you compare the two.</Callout>

## Choose a format and topic

1. Choose **Listen and repeat / Guided speaking / Free speaking**.
2. Choose **Topic from my keywords** or **Enter a topic directly**. If keywords are unavailable, select some in learning settings or enter a topic yourself.
3. In **Session settings**, check the correction mode, target duration, AI voice, and playback speed. Free speaking also offers optional conversation goals and an AI role.
4. Click **Start Speaking (Speaking 시작)** and wait for the question or conversation situation to be prepared.

Read-aloud and guided speaking first show an AI sentence or guide. If a session is already in progress, use **Continue learning**. If the app could not check for an ongoing session, click **Check again** first.

## Send a recorded answer

1. Click **Request microphone permission** and allow it in the browser.
2. Read the sentence, guide, or AI question, then click **Start recording**.
3. When you finish, click **Stop recording**, then **Preview my recording**. Use **Record again** to replace it.
4. Click **Send answer**. After speech recognition and AI response preparation finish, check the transcript, conversation, and AI audio.

A normal conversation recording must be at least one second and can be at most 60 seconds. If a time or file-size limit appears, make a shorter recording. Leaving the screen during recording may lose the current recording.

## Listen and repeat

Practice today's five questions in order. Check each question's AI audio and script, and say **the same sentence at least twice**. You can add a third attempt if needed.

Replace a misrecognized attempt using that attempt's **Record again** button. When valid attempts are ready, click **Evaluate this question and continue**. For the last question, click **Evaluate the final question**. Each question shows whether evaluation is waiting, running, complete, or failed.

## Guided speaking, free speaking, and assistance

In guided speaking, use the facts and required content to form your own response. In free speaking, talk with AI about the topic. You can write keywords or an outline in **My notes**. Notes are automatically saved as a draft, but are not sent for AI evaluation.

If you need help, use **Learning assistance** for replay, slower playback, reviewing the question, hints, translation, and sample answers. Assistance use is saved with the learning record, so review it alongside the result. Distinguish AI examples from what you actually said.

## End the session and review results

When **End and get coaching** is offered, the app prepares coaching from saved utterances. This coaching does not contribute to an overall score or ability profile.

For a session that offers scored evaluation, check **Speaking evaluation readiness** and choose **End and get evaluated**. If conditions are not met, continue speaking or choose **End without evaluation**. Ending without evaluation preserves history and study time, but does not add a score or update the learning profile. You cannot add answers to an ended session, so check before finishing.

Watch the status while evaluation or coaching is prepared. If it fails, use the result screen's retry button. Reopen results and conversations under **Speaking** in [learning history](/en/language-learning/history).

## If speech recognition is wrong

First, listen to your own recording. Recognized text can differ from what you said. Record again if needed, or use **Report STT error** to describe the error type and actual utterance. Read the consent information about retaining the original audio. For more help, use **Request customer support** after the report is submitted.

If only one processing stage fails, use the offered action, such as **Retry failed stage** or **Regenerate TTS only**. If you exclude an utterance from evaluation, it remains in the record but does not affect the score; read the confirmation before continuing.

## Check that you are done

Check your answer and its processing status on the result screen. If evaluation is still running, read the feedback when it finishes. You can reopen saved results in learning history.
