## Find a work

1. Sign in with an administrator account and open [Web novels](/en/novel) in the sidebar.
2. Select a platform such as **Kakuyomu**.
3. Search by title or browse genres and rankings. If a ranking offers a period selector, pick the period you want.
4. Open a work and select an episode in its contents. A short story may open directly in the reader.

## Change the order of the results

In a Kakuyomu list, use **Sort by** to switch between **Ready first** and **Original rank / relevance**. Source information may appear first, with translated titles or descriptions appearing as they become available. When the retry button is available, you can retry a failed work individually.

For English translation, see [Use English across TranslaCat](/en/docs/english-support). On Kakuyomu, use **Translation language** in the list or reader to select Korean or English.

Continue with [Translate and listen to an episode](/en/docs/novel/read-listen).
