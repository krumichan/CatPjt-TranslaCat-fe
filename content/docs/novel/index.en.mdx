<Callout title="Administrator access">
  Anyone can read this guide. The novel menu and reader are available to administrator accounts only.
</Callout>

Open [Novel translation](/en/novel), select a platform and choose a work. In the reader, you can read the original Japanese, view the available translation or listen to the current episode.

<Cards>
  <Card title="Find novels and browse rankings" href="/en/docs/novel/explore">Search by title, browse genres and rankings, and choose a sort order.</Card>
  <Card title="Translate and listen to an episode" href="/en/docs/novel/read-listen">Translate the episode, open the controls and play the text aloud.</Card>
</Cards>
