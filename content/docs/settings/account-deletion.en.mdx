## Open the deletion page

From the profile menu at the top right, open [**Settings**](/en/settings). In the account card at the top, select **Account and data deletion (계정 탈퇴·데이터 삭제)**. Read what is deleted and what remains on the [deletion page](/en/account-deletion). To make the request, sign in with the account you intend to delete.

The deletion button and confirmation page currently use Korean labels, including on the English site. The Korean labels below help you find each control.

<Callout title="An accepted deletion request cannot be undone">
  Once the request is accepted, you are signed out on all devices. If you only want to add a sign-in method, see the [account and sign-in guide](/en/docs/settings/account-login).
</Callout>

## What is deleted and what remains

- Your profile, linked accounts, friendships and blocks, device connections, private inquiries and learning records, personal account books, and saved personal audio are deleted.
- Shared chat messages and translations remain, with the author shown as **Deleted user**. Shared account books pass to another participant and retain shared records with your identity anonymized.
- Moderation evidence is visible only to the moderation team and is deleted **90 days after the report was submitted**.

## Verify your identity and request deletion

1. Check that **Current TranslaCat ID (현재 TranslaCat ID)** is the account you intend to delete.
2. Reverify your linked Google account or use your current password for **Identity verification (본인 확인)**. Complete the final confirmation within five minutes of verifying.
3. Read the deletion and retention details and select the checkbox. Enter your current TranslaCat ID exactly, then select **Delete account and request data deletion (계정 탈퇴하고 데이터 삭제 요청)**.

## Check the status after submission

**Deletion in progress (삭제 처리 중)** means the service is still cleaning up data and files. Use **Refresh status (처리 상태 새로고침)** in the same browser tab. You can use the stored receipt to check the status for seven days after completion. Keep the tab's stored data or the app's data until you confirm completion. Clearing the receipt does not cancel an accepted request.
