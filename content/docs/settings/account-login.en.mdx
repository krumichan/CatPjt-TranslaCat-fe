Connect another sign-in method to your existing account. Check whether you currently use an email account or a Google account, then follow the matching steps.

<Callout title="Before you begin">You can read this guide without signing in. Sign in to make changes with your own account. Service links open the English interface. Screenshots show the Korean interface with example data; Korean labels in parentheses help you compare the two.</Callout>

## Open account and sign-in

Click **Account and login (계정 및 로그인)** at the top of [Settings](/en/settings). The [account screen](/en/settings/account) shows your email and available sign-in methods.

## Connect Google to an email account

1. While signed in with email and password, open **Account and login**.
2. Enter your **current password** in the Google section.
3. Click **Verify password and link Google**, then choose a Google account with **the same email address** shown on screen.
4. Return to the account screen and check that Google is **Available**.

After connecting, Google can sign in to the same account. If you cancel Google account selection or an error occurs, read the notice and retry.

## Add email sign-in to a Google account

1. In your Google-signed-in account, open **Account and login**.
2. Under email sign-in, click **Verify Google account again** and verify your own Google account.
3. After returning, request an email verification code and verify the email with the received code.
4. Enter and confirm a new password, then click **Set password**. Use at least eight characters and follow the field instructions.
5. Check that email sign-in is **Available**.

Complete email verification and password registration **within five minutes** after Google reverification. If the time expires, begin with Google verification again. An account that already has email sign-in shows it as available.

## Check that you are done

Check that the added method is **Available**. If connecting fails, check the current password, Google email address, and displayed error. If the registration window expired, repeat Google verification first.
