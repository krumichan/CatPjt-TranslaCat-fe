---
title: Change display language and theme
description: Choose an application display language and switch between light and dark themes.
---

Change the language used for application buttons and menus, or the screen's light/dark theme. These settings are separate from chat translation languages and your study language.

## Find the controls on your device

<Tabs items={["PC", "Mobile"]} groupId="guide-device-en">
  <Tab value="PC">
    1. Open the **Language** button in the service header. It shows a flag or **EN**. Choose **EN (English)** for English menus and buttons.
    2. Use the adjacent **sun/moon button** to switch between light and dark themes.

    <GuideScreenshot src="/images/guides/ko/language-pc.jpg" alt="Language menu and theme control in the service header" title="PC: use the header controls" caption="Korean interface with example data.   </Tab>
  <Tab value="Mobile">
    1. Open the **☰ menu** at the top left.
    2. Open the **Language** button at the bottom of the menu and choose **EN (English)**. The button shows a flag or **EN**.
    3. Use the **theme button** in the same area to change the screen theme.

## English guides and English study

The service offers **Korean, Japanese, English, and Learning mode** interfaces. Open the **Language** menu and choose **EN (English)** to use English menus and buttons. Study and translation languages are separate settings; choosing the English interface does not change those targets.

The screenshots show a Korean-interface example. Choose **EN** in the Language menu for English menus and buttons. The guide's language selector opens the English documentation at **/en/docs**. It is separate from the application's Language menu. Use [learning settings](/en/docs/language-learning/settings-history) to study English or [chat defaults](/en/docs/settings/chat-defaults) to read English translations.

## Check that you are done

Check that the menu language or background theme changed. Use the mobile instructions if your PC window is narrow. [Getting started](/en/docs/getting-started) also shows the controls on each device.
