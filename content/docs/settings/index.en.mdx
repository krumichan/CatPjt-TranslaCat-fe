## Settings that are not available yet

The **General settings**, **Voice translation settings**, and **Account book settings** cards show **Coming soon**. **AI voice reading settings** shows **Planned**. These cards cannot currently be opened. Change voice translation languages and recording options in the translation feature itself; manage account book categories and goals inside an account book.

For study languages, keywords, and web AI integration, see the [learning settings guide](/en/docs/language-learning/settings-history).

## If you need help

If a screen will not load, use its reload action. If the app says your sign-in expired, sign in again. Each task's completion check explains how to confirm saving or uploading. For unresolved issues, see the [customer support guide](/en/docs/support). Describe the screen and error without including passwords or verification codes.
