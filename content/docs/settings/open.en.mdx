Open the screens for your personal information and service defaults.

<Callout title="Before you begin">You can read this guide without signing in. Sign in to make changes with your own account. Service links open the English interface. Screenshots show the Korean interface with example data; Korean labels in parentheses help you compare the two.</Callout>

## Start from the profile menu

<Tabs items={["PC", "Mobile"]} groupId="guide-device-en">
  <Tab value="PC">
    1. Click the **person-shaped button at the top right**.
    2. Choose **My profile (내 프로필)** for your name and introduction, or **Settings (설정)** for sign-in methods and chat defaults.
    3. The **Manage profile** and **Account and login** buttons at the top of Settings also open the related screens.

    <GuideScreenshot src="/images/guides/ko/settings-pc.jpg" alt="Desktop settings screen with the user profile menu open" title="PC: use the top-right profile menu" caption="Korean interface with example data. Choose My profile or Settings from the profile menu to open the screen you need." width={1280} height={900} step={1} locale="en" />
  </Tab>
  <Tab value="Mobile">
    1. Tap the **person-shaped button at the top right**. It is separate from the service menu on the left.
    2. Choose **My profile (내 프로필)** or **Settings (설정)**.
    3. Scroll through the settings cards to find an item. The change procedure is the same as on PC.

    <GuideScreenshot src="/images/guides/ko/settings-mobile.jpg" alt="Mobile settings screen with the top-right user menu open" title="Mobile: open settings at the top right" caption="Korean interface with example data. The profile button stays at the top right on mobile. Settings cards continue vertically below it." width={390} height={844} step={1} locale="en" />
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Check that you are done

Profile opens a preview and editing area; Settings opens account information and settings cards. Your TranslaCat ID is also available on these screens.
