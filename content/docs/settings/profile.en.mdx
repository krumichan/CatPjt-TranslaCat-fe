Change your nickname and introduction, and find the unique ID used for friend requests and account book invitations.

<Callout title="Before you begin">You can read this guide without signing in. Sign in to make changes with your own account. Service links open the English interface. Screenshots show the Korean interface with example data; Korean labels in parentheses help you compare the two.</Callout>

## Change your nickname and introduction

1. Open [My profile](/en/settings/profile).
2. Under **Edit profile (프로필 수정)**, enter a **nickname (닉네임)** and **Bio (자기소개)**. A nickname is required and can be at most 30 characters; the introduction can be at most 200 characters.
3. Click **Save (저장)** and check the **Your profile has been saved** confirmation.

**Cancel** restores your edits to the last loaded values. On PC, the preview and editor appear side by side. On mobile, scroll below the preview to reach the editor.

## Find your TranslaCat ID

Find **TranslaCat ID** in Profile or at the top of [Settings](/en/settings). Use its copy button to copy it. This unique ID is used for adding friends and inviting account book members. Unlike your nickname, it cannot be changed on this screen.

## Check that you are done

Check the save confirmation and the name and introduction in your profile preview. If you copied your ID to share for an invitation, distinguish it from your nickname.

If Save is disabled, check whether anything changed or a save is already running. If the profile failed to load, use **Reload**. Sign in again if the app says your session expired.
