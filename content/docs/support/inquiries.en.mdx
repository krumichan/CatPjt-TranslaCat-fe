---
title: Create a private inquiry
description: Ask a question about the service or report a problem in a private inquiry.
---

Start by [opening Support](/en/docs/support/open).

## Follow these steps

1. Select **1:1 inquiries → Contact support (1:1 문의 → 문의하기)**.
2. Under **Category (분류)**, choose General usage, Account, Translation, Language learning, Chat, Account books, or Other, as appropriate.
3. Enter the **Title (제목)** and **Content (내용)**. The title can contain up to 200 characters and the content up to 10,000 characters.

4. Select **Submit inquiry (문의 등록)**. The inquiry details show the content and its status.

Describe the feature and steps you used, the result you expected, what actually happened, the time, and any message shown. Include whether you were on PC or mobile, your browser, and the language you were using.

<Callout title="Who can read an inquiry?">
  Only you and administrators can view your private inquiry. Do not include passwords or verification codes. Include useful details such as the error message and steps to reproduce the issue.
</Callout>

## Check the result

Check the title, content, and status in the submitted inquiry. Read replies and ask follow-up questions in the same inquiry.
