Start by [opening Support](/en/docs/support/open).

## Follow these steps

1. Select **Notices (공지사항)** in Support.
2. Select a **Title (제목)** in the list to open the notice.
3. After reading, select **Back to list (목록으로)**.

## When an urgent notice appears

**Urgent notices (긴급 공지)** may also appear in the notification center and a dialog after sign-in. **Close (닫기)** dismisses the notice for the current signed-in session. **Don't show again (그만 보기)** saves your preference to hide it in future sessions as well. If more urgent notices remain, the next one appears. If a message says the preference could not be saved, try again.

## Check the result

You should be able to read the notice's title and body and return to the list. For an urgent notice, choose Close or Don't show again as appropriate.
