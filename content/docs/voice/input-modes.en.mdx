## Choose an input mode

| Mode | When to use it | Permissions and settings |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Microphone (마이크)** | Translate what you say | Allow microphone access. Results are labeled **My voice (내 음성)**. |
| **System audio (시스템 오디오)** | Translate audio from a video or online meeting | Select the tab or screen playing the sound and enable audio sharing. Results are labeled **Other person's voice (상대방 음성)**. |
| **Meeting (회의)** | Translate your voice and the other person's audio together | Allow both tab/screen audio sharing and microphone access. The inputs are labeled **My voice / Other person's voice (내 음성 / 상대방 음성)**. |

When sharing system audio, select **the tab actually playing Teams, YouTube, or the other audio source**. Selecting the TranslaCat tab may not capture the other person's sound. Also enable **Share tab audio** in the browser's sharing dialog. The session cannot start if the shared screen contains no audio.

In Meeting mode, headphones help reduce the other person's audio being picked up again by your microphone.

## Prepare permissions on your device

<Tabs items={["PC", "Mobile"]} groupId="guide-device-en">
  <Tab value="PC">
    In the tab/screen sharing dialog, choose the tab playing the sound and enable **Share tab audio**. Meeting mode also needs microphone permission.
  </Tab>
  <Tab value="Mobile">
    Try **Microphone (마이크)** mode first. If the browser does not support tab or screen sharing with audio, System audio and Meeting modes are unavailable. Use a supported PC browser for those modes.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Check the result

Check that the chosen mode matches the sound you want to translate and that you can grant all required permissions.
