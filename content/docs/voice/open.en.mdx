You can read this guide without signing in. To use the feature, [sign in](/en/login) to your own account.

Open the [voice translation screen](/en/voice) to follow along. Service links open the English interface. The screenshots remain Korean-interface references.

## Open voice translation

<Tabs items={["PC", "Mobile"]} groupId="guide-device-en">
  <Tab value="PC">
    1. After signing in, select **Voice translation (음성 번역)** in the left menu, or open the [voice translation screen](/en/voice).
    2. Choose a mode and languages under **Translation session setup (번역 세션 설정)**. On a wide screen, input modes appear in a row and language settings in two columns.

  </Tab>
  <Tab value="Mobile">
    1. After signing in, open **☰ menu → Voice translation (음성 번역)** at the top left, or open the [voice translation screen](/en/voice).
    2. The settings are stacked vertically. Scroll down to check the languages and storage option.
    3. Try **Microphone (마이크)** mode first. System audio and Meeting require a browser that supports **tab or screen sharing with audio**. If your device does not support it, use a PC.

    <GuideScreenshot

</Tabs>

The menu and layout change with the available width. In a narrow PC window, follow the Mobile instructions.

## Check the result

You are ready when the translation session setup shows the input mode and language settings.
